# Community guidelines

This project welcomes thoughtful technical criticism, accessible design feedback and reproducible reports from people of different backgrounds and experience.

- Discuss the work and evidence rather than attacking a person.
- Be clear about what was observed, inferred, proposed or still untested.
- Respect privacy, attribution and the licenses of shared work.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation and publishing another person's private information.
- Keep discussions relevant and leave space for disagreement and correction.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove inappropriate content and limit participation when needed to protect the project community. These guidelines apply to project issues, discussions, reviews and contributions.

Use GitHub's **Report content** tools for abuse, spam or platform-safety concerns; do not repost personal details as evidence in a public thread. Security vulnerabilities use the project's [private reporting channel](SECURITY.md), not public issues. For a moderation question, request a private contact without including sensitive details publicly.
