Keep the target in view

Project, worktree, session, state, read age and usage share the display. A retained view is visibly distinct from fresh authority.

B

Tap a session key

Number, title, provider and state stay together. Color and symbols help you find work without reading every row.

C

Choose an explicit command

Open full content, prepare input, review a reusable phrase or open the list. Unsupported responses stay disabled.

D

Navigate by touch

The stick and whole-surface swipes move through the supported tab/project order. The stick center opens content.

E

Hold to make a voice draft

Release to finish capture. Transcribed words remain editable; speaking never grants approval or sends automatically.

F

Find what needs attention

The dial moves between attention items. In the content sheet, a separate dial scrolls the reader rather than changing the target.
